THE THREE WALLS

WALL MARIA

Outer Wall | Height: 50m

The second line of defense. Breached at Trost but successfully sealed, marking humanity's first victory.

The innermost wall, housing the King, nobles, and the Military Police. Deepest safety within Paradis.

GLOBAL POWERS

THE WORLD'S NATIONS

MARLEY

The dominant global superpower. Utilizes the Power of the Titans through their Warrior Unit to conquer other nations, while keeping Eldians in internment zones.

PARADIS

The isolated island nation where King Fritz fled. Demonized by the world as the "Island of Devils," sitting on massive reserves of Iceburst Stone.

HIZURU

An allied nation of the Eldian Empire prior to the Great Titan War. Now seeking to re-establish ties with Paradis to monopolize Iceburst Stone.

HISTORY OF THE WORLD

ORIGIN OF TITANS

King Karl Fritz plots with the Tybur family to orchestrate Marley's rise and the collapse of the Eldian Empire.

FOUNDING OF WALLS

King Fritz retreats to Paradis, using millions of Colossal Titans to build three concentric walls.

FALL OF SHIGANSHINA

Year 845. Marley sends the Warrior Unit to breach Wall Maria and reclaim the Founding Titan.
